# GAYATRI PLATFORM v4.0.0 — INSTITUTION DEMO SCRIPT & PRESENTATION GUIDE

**Audience:** University Academic Councils, Department Chairs, Engineering Faculty, Deans of Academics.  
**Demonstration Duration:** 10–15 minutes.  
**Key Goal:** Prove that Gayatri is a course-agnostic adaptive learning platform with institution-owned knowledge.

---

## Act 1: The Vision — Beyond Chatbots (2 Minutes)
- **Opening Statement:** *"Current cloud chatbots spoon-feed answers to students, encouraging superficial learning, risking academic integrity, and requiring constant internet connectivity. Gayatri is designed from first principles as an institution-owned, Socratic adaptive learning platform."*
- **Showcase Screen A:** Highlight:
  - Course-Independent Architecture
  - Strictly Grounded Knowledge (Scoped RAG)
  - 100% Offline Air-Gapped Data Privacy

---

## Act 2: Course Independence & Context Isolation (4 Minutes)
- **Step 1:** Select **Engineering Mathematics (MATH201)**.
- **Prompt:** *"Explain first-order differential equations and integrating factor."*
- **Action:** Open the **Retrieved Evidence** side drawer. Show the faculty the atomic concept card from their textbook.
- **Step 2:** Click **Course Catalog** $\to$ Switch to **Digital Electronics (EC202)**.
- **Prompt:** *"Explain De Morgan's theorem."*
- **Key Talking Point:** Show that the identical tutoring engine now retrieves Boolean algebra truth tables, with zero residual mathematical leakage from the previous session.

---

## Act 3: Socratic Pedagogy & Misconception Remediation (4 Minutes)
- **Talking Point:** *"What happens when a student makes a fundamental reasoning error?"*
- **Input:** *"For a NAND gate, the output is 0 when any input is 0."*
- **Observe:**
  - The Socratic engine flags the **NAND/NOR Logic Inversion Error**.
  - It does not mark the student wrong and dump the final table; it guides the student through the inverted logic of AND followed by NOT.
  - Navigate to **Student Mastery**: Show that the cognitive state recorded the misconception and updated the recommended remediation.

---

## Act 4: Institutional Value — Teacher Copilot & Custom Notes (3 Minutes)
- **Step 1:** Click **Teacher Copilot**.
  - Show the class performance heatmap.
  - Point to the alert: *"28.5% of students showed NAND inversion errors during problem checks."*
  - Explain how faculty receive actionable pedagogical intervention recommendations before class.
- **Step 2:** Click **Bring Curriculum**.
  - Explain that university departments can upload custom lecture notes (PDF/DOCX) to immediately instantiate course-grounded tutors without custom code.
